# Record fulfillment and retain experience

Apply [conversation.md](conversation.md) and [escalation.md](escalation.md). Accept natural updates about delivery or service completion and resolve the related order from context.

## Required record

Capture actual arrival/completion date, quantities or accepted service scope, condition, remaining shortages, and acceptance evidence where required. Keep arrival, receipt entry, inspection, and technical acceptance distinct. Partial fulfillment leaves outstanding work visible.

Compare actual timing with the original and revised vendor promises. Record discrepancies and their resolver. Any return, replacement, or changed commitment follows applicable authority.

## Knowledge contribution

Link the outcome to the vendor, category, source, and purchase. Preserve attributed feedback separately from observed facts. Update lead-time summaries without counting partial receipts as separate completed orders. Missing optional feedback does not prevent closure.

## Done when

Fulfillment and any remaining work are recorded, the correct completion state is returned, and the next relevant request can retrieve the experience. Simulated receipts remain distinguishable from actual supplier performance.
